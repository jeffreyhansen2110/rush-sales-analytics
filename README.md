

# RUSH Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data for RUSH, a global sportswear and footwear company. The goal was to clean and prepare raw sales data, answer key business questions from the VP of US Sales, and identify additional trends and insights.

## Data Sources
The analysis uses three raw data tables:

- TABLE_SALES_885.csv
- TABLE_RETAILER_885.csv
- TABLE_PRODUCTS_885.csv

## Key Business Questions
The analysis addresses the following questions:

- What product category had the highest sales in 2021?
- What state had the highest sales of women's products in 2021?
- What state had the highest sales of men's products in 2021?
- What retailer purchased the most units in 2021 and 2020?

## Key Findings
- Men's Street Footwear had the highest 2021 sales at $74,667,440.
- Maine had the highest 2021 sales of women's products at $2,176,301.
- New Hampshire had the highest 2021 sales of men's products at $5,420,808.
- Amazon purchased the most units in 2020 with 317,930 units.
- Foot Locker purchased the most units in 2021 with 1,097,410 units.
- Total sales increased approximately 510.5% from 2020 to 2021.
- Online sales grew from approximately $4.5 million in 2020 to $92.4 million in 2021.
